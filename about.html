THE STORY

WHY THIS EXISTS

Every semester starts the same way: neat notebooks, big plans, colour-coded everything. And every semester ends with notes scattered across five apps, three devices and one notebook I can no longer read.

So I did the obvious thing a CS student would do — I built my own corner of the internet for it. Every subject gets a page. Every chapter gets its own page. The notes are written directly in HTML, which means they load instantly, work on any device, and can't get lost in a folder called final_final_v2_REAL.

It's called LOKESHVERSE because "Lokesh's notes" was too boring to survive.

IMPORTANT

These notes are written by a student, for students. They're how I understand things — clear enough to revise from, honest enough to admit when something is a simplification. If something looks wrong, it probably is; tell me and I'll fix it.
