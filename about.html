Hi, I'm Lokesh — a Computer Science student who likes building things, participating in hackathons, and turning random ideas into something that actually works.

LOKESHVERSE started as my own study system. I kept writing notes anyway — the only problem was that they lived in messy files only I could navigate. So I built a place where every subject gets a page, every chapter gets a page, and everything looks like it belongs in a zine instead of a PDF graveyard.

Then I thought, why keep them to myself? So this became a public notes space — what I'm learning, chapter by chapter, free forever.

THE RULES OF THE VERSE

WHAT THIS PLACE IS

📝 NOTES, NOT PDFs

Every note is handwritten HTML. They load instantly on any connection, work offline once cached, and look the same everywhere.

🗂️ CHAPTER BY CHAPTER

University → course → semester → subject → chapter. No dumping grounds. You always know exactly where you are.

🚀 STUDENT BUILT

No frameworks, no trackers, no corporate LMS energy. Just one CSS file, a little JavaScript, and too much chai.
